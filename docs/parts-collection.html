The last table is what the photos and caliper readings have to answer, because those answers set the footprints and therefore the board size. Quantities include spares. The 19 V→12 V stage is the XL4016E1 module, so the board carries no power inductor, no rectifier for that stage, and no buck IC.

3. What the photos and calipers have to answer

3.1 The nine resistors — the biggest lever on board area

The design assumes DIN0204: 3.6 mm body, 1.6 mm Ø, 5.08 mm pad pitch. If the cabinet's are 0207 (≈5.5 × 2.5 mm, leads spread to ≈10 mm) every resistor takes twice the room. Report body length, body Ø, and the spacing the leads land at when the part sits flat.

3.2 The disc caps

Assumed Ø3.8 / P2.50 for the 100 n and Ø3.0 / P2.50 for the 100 p. A 104 disc is usually Ø5–6 mm. Report body Ø, thickness, and lead spread where the leads exit the body.

3.3 Headers — the cabinet has no 4‑pin strip

Three ways to make J2 and J6: snap 5‑pin strips down to 4; salvage the NUC's own fan plug and solder its wires into a 4‑pin header; or solder fan wires straight into the pads. The JST‑XH 4P housings in L1 E9 are 2.5 mm pitch and will not sit on 2.54 mm pins. Report whether the strips are breakaway.

3.4 D2's span decides the two fan rails

The footprint spreads its pads 10.16 mm apart. A DO‑41 body is ≈5.3 × 2.7 mm with ≈2.7 mm natural lead spacing, so the leads can be bent out to 5.08 mm and sit flat. Report body length and Ø.

3.5 The buck module decides J6

Some XL4016 modules have screw terminals (5.08 mm pitch, no pins at all), some have plain pins. Report module L × W, what the input and output terminals are and their spacing, where the two trim pots sit, whether it has mounting holes, and how tall its tallest part is.

3.6 The regulators

Confirm both AMS1117s are SOT‑223 (TO‑252). If the 5 V one is TO‑220 instead, L1 B1 also holds L7805CV (TO‑220, 9 pcs) and BA033ST (TO‑220 3.3 V, 4 pcs) — a deliberate all‑THT option, at the cost of two TO‑220 footprints on a board that is trying to be small.

3.7 The transistors

TO‑92 leads come flat (one row) or staggered (middle lead offset); the design uses TO-92_Inline_Wide. Report which the BC547s are and confirm the marking reads BC547B. Pin order facing the flat face: 1 = E, 2 = B, 3 = C.

3.8 The Pro Mini

Confirm 3.3 V / 8 MHz (crystal marked 8.000). The board's 3.3 V rail and the firmware expect it.

4. Not in the cabinet

M3 mounting hardware for the board corners (four holes, if they stay) plus four nuts, and hookup wire. The 19 V lead, its fuse, and the NUC's 5 V tap are bench items, not board items.
